How it works

Three steps from signup to a decision

  1. 1

    Create a session

    Your backend calls one endpoint with the verification mode and your own reference id. You get back a unique link.

  2. 2

    Your user verifies

    They open the link on their phone, photograph the document and, in selfie mode, take a live selfie. No app to install, no front-end work for you.

  3. 3

    Receive a signed decision

    A webhook arrives with approved, declined or review, machine-readable reasons and 0..1 scores. Or poll the session whenever you like.

Verification modes

Pick the level of assurance you need

Two modes, one API. Supported documents: passport, national ID card and driver licence.

0.04 USDT / check

Document check

One photo of the document. Fast, low friction, ideal as a first gate.

  • Data extraction: name, date of birth, document number, nationality, expiry
  • MRZ checksum validation for passports and ID cards with an MRZ
  • Tamper signals: screen captures, prints and edited documents
  • Optional match against the name you expect
0.08 USDT / check

Selfie + Document

Everything in the document check, plus proof that the person holding the phone is the person on the document.

  • Face match between the document photo and a live selfie
  • Passive liveness: anti-spoofing against printed photos and screen replays
  • No head-turn or video required from the user
  • All document-check signals included

Result in 1–2 seconds for documents with a valid MRZ. Other documents read by the AI typically finish in 10–30 seconds with automatic failover. PilotKYC reads the front side or data page of the document.

Security & privacy

Privacy by design, not as an add-on

The best way to protect personal data is to not keep it. PilotKYC keeps as little as possible, for as short as possible.

Images deleted right after the check

Photos are processed and removed as soon as the check ends. They are never written to a database.

Short retention, erasable on demand

Extracted personal data is kept at most 30 days by default, and you can erase it through the API at any time.

Signed webhooks

Every webhook carries an HMAC-SHA256 signature with a timestamp, so you can reject forgeries and replays.

Keys stored as hashes

API keys are stored only as hashes. The plaintext is shown once, at creation. Keys are server-side only.

Safe failure mode

If recognition fails or times out, the result is review with a reason code. A technical error is never turned into an approval.

Nothing leaks to the browser

The hosted page never shows the decision, scores or extracted data to the browser. Only your server learns the outcome.

Developer experience

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which documents are supported?

Passports, national ID cards and driver licences. PilotKYC reads the front side or the data page of the document.

How fast is a result?

1–2 seconds for documents with a valid MRZ. Other documents read by the AI typically finish in 10–30 seconds with automatic provider failover. You are notified by webhook as soon as the decision is ready, or you can poll the session.

Do you store my users' photos?

No. Images are processed and deleted right after the check and are never written to a database. Extracted personal data is kept at most 30 days by default and can be erased through the API at any time.

What does review mean?

No hard failure was found, but at least one signal was not strong enough to approve automatically. You decide: queue it for a human, or ask the user to try again. Reason codes tell you why.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. Sandbox keys are free and return deterministic outcomes, so you can build and test the full flow, including webhooks.

Do you do AML or sanctions screening?

Not in v1. PilotKYC verifies a document and, optionally, a face. AML and sanctions screening, proof of address and KYB are on the roadmap, with no committed dates.

Sign up in the dashboard, confirm your email and create API keys yourself: sandbox keys are free, live keys use your prepaid balance. Sign up

Live verifications cost 0.04 USDT for Document check (mode=document) and 0.08 USDT for Selfie + Document (mode=selfie_document), charged only when a decision is produced; if our engine fails, the check is refunded automatically.
